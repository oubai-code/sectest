About Proyecto TNR CDMX 🌸

Proyecto TNR CDMX is dedicated to animal welfare in Mexico City through Trap-Neuter-Return (TNR) initiatives, responsible adoptions, and community care.

We work tirelessly to reduce street animal overpopulation humanely, provide medical treatment, and match rescued cats and dogs with loving forever families.
